KEEP IT SIMPLE

Your tools.
Your decisions.

The kit gives you a starting point.
You stay in charge of the week.

Which AI assistant can I use?

Any assistant that accepts a text prompt. Copy and paste, or download a .txt file. No model API or new subscription is required by this website. Your chosen assistant has its own access rules and limits.

Does this connect to my calendar or task apps?

No. This page prepares prompts. Your AI can use a connector only if it actually has one and you authorize it. Otherwise, paste a short summary and use the resulting checklist. The prompts ask for a preview and approval before any external changes.

What are the defaults?

A 10-hour example budget, a 20% unscheduled buffer, focus blocks up to 50 minutes with 10-minute breaks, one main outcome per day, and a brief daily close and weekly review. All supporting time must fit inside your available windows. Change these starting points to suit your week.

What happens to the context I enter?

The customizer processes planning entries only in this tab. Only your language and theme choices are saved on this device. The page has no account, analytics collector or AI connection. Your host handles page requests under its own policies. Copying a prompt into an AI shares that text with the provider you choose. Leave out secrets and confidential information.

Can I use and adapt the prompts?

Yes, for your personal or internal business planning. This permission covers the six prompt texts, not the rest of the repository or third-party brands. AI output needs your review; this kit promises no particular result.
